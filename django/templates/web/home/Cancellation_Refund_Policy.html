Cancellation & Refund Policy

At SSC, we strive to provide a seamless experience for our users. However, we understand that situations may arise where a cancellation or refund is necessary. Our policy is designed to be transparent and fair to ensure clarity in all transactions.

Cancellation Policy

Customers may request a cancellation within 24 hours of making a payment. In such cases, a full refund will be issued, excluding any applicable transaction or processing fees. Cancellation requests must be sent via email to support@sscegov.com, including the payment reference number and a brief explanation. Our team will review the request and respond within three business days. Cancellations requested after 24 hours of payment are generally not eligible for a refund unless there are exceptional circumstances such as technical errors or duplicate transactions.

Refund Policy

If SSC cancels a service due to operational or technical reasons, customers will receive a full refund, which will be processed automatically within seven to ten business days. Refunds are typically credited back to the original payment method used during the transaction. Customers should note that processing times may vary depending on the bank or payment gateway, and any transaction fees incurred are non-refundable. SSC does not provide refunds for cancellations made beyond the permitted timeframe, failure to meet eligibility criteria, or non-utilization of services.

Modification & Transfer Policy

Payments and registrations are non-transferable. However, users may request modifications to their registration details up to three days before their scheduled service, subject to approval. Requests for modifications must be sent via email, and changes are subject to availability and verification.

Policy Updates & Contact Information

We encourage all users to review the terms and conditions before making a payment. SSC reserves the right to update this policy at any time without prior notice. For further assistance regarding cancellations, refunds, or modifications, customers can contact us via email at samatwaservicecenter[dot]gov[dot]in[at]gmail[dot]com or by phone during business hours.
