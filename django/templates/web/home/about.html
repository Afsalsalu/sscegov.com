Samatwa Service Centre E-Governance Service

Samatwa Service Centers (SSC) are essential to the Digital India Plan, ensuring efficient delivery of e-governance and commercial services to citizens in both rural and urban areas. SSC acts as a bridge between the government and the public, offering a wide range of services such as access to government schemes, social welfare programs, financial services, online education, healthcare, and agricultural support. With a presence in all 36 states and union territories, SSC operates in over 765 districts, enhancing digital accessibility and empowering individuals. By promoting innovation and inclusivity, SSC is redefining service delivery to make governance accessible to all.

SSC ACADEMY

SSC Academy is an initiative of the Common Services Centers (SSC) program under the Government of India. It provides digital literacy, skill development, and professional training programs to empower citizens, especially in rural and semi-urban areas. The academy offers courses in IT, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, cybersecurity, and various vocational skills, helping individuals improve their employability and business opportunities. Through online and offline learning, SSC Academy aims to bridge the digital divide and promote inclusive education. It collaborates with government agencies, private organizations, and educational institutions to deliver high-qualitytraining.

SSC TOURISM

SSC Tourism is a dedicated service under the SAMATWA SERVICE CENTRE (SSC) initiative, designed to promote travel and tourism across India. This platform aims to make travel more accessible and affordable, particularly for citizens in rural and semi-urban areas. Through SSC Tourism, individuals can explore a wide range of travel options, including budget-friendly tour packages, transportation services, accommodation assistance, and guided tours to some of India's most breathtaking destinations. By leveraging a seamless booking experience and local travel support, SSC Tourism ensures that every traveler enjoys a hassle-free journey. Our mission is to bridge the gap between urban and rural travel accessibility, empowering communities to experience the beauty and diversity of India like never before. Whether its a pilgrimage, a cultural retreat, or a leisure vacation, SSC Tourism is here to make your travel dreams a reality. Discover. Explore. Experience with SSC Tourism!

ONLINE TICKET BOOKING

Samatwa Service Centers (SSCs) are part of the Digital India initiative and provide various online booking services for citizens. These centers help people book tickets for travel, government services, and other appointments without needing a personal internet connection or digital literacy.
